Lantern & Ladle is a night-market cooking game set during a three-minute shift. Chop toppings, watch two burners, and layer each dish in recipe order through Opening, Dinner Rush, and Last Call.

## Run it

Run `node serve.ts` and open localhost on port 8080.

## How to play

- Press **Enter** to open the stall.
- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. Press **E** or **Space** to use a nearby station. Hold either key to chop.
- Click a station to walk to it and use it. Click a ticket or press **Q** to focus an order. Press **T** to toggle the route to its next layer.
- Use both burners to overlap cooking. Lift each ingredient when it is ready and before its countdown ends. The plating counter can hold one finished dish while the serving window is occupied.
- Serve VIP orders before their bonus clocks run out for extra tips. Consecutive orders served raise your tip multiplier up to ×3. An expired order resets the streak.
- Press **R** for recipes, **P** to pause or resume, and **M** to toggle sound.
