Lantern & Ladle is a night-market cooking game set during a three-minute dinner rush. Chop toppings, watch the stove, and assemble ingredients in recipe order to serve dishes before their tickets expire.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080 (or play game/index.html).
```

## How to play

- Press **Enter** to open the stall.
- Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. Press **E** or **Space** to use a nearby station, and hold either key to chop.
- Click a station to walk to it and use it.
- Press **R** for recipes, **P** to pause or resume, and **M** to toggle sound.
